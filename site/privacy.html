Privacy Policy

Tyto for macOS · Last updated 17 September 2026

The short version

Tyto collects nothing. It makes no network connections of any kind. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no server. Nothing you capture is transmitted anywhere.

What stays on your Mac

  • Captures. Images you take go to your clipboard and, if you turn that option on, to a folder you choose. They are ordinary files on your own disk.
  • Settings. Your shortcut, default tool, colour, thickness and save location are stored locally by macOS in Tyto's own container.
  • Recent captures. If enabled, Tyto remembers the file paths of your recent captures so it can list them in the menu bar. The list is local.

Permissions

Tyto asks for Screen Recording permission because macOS requires it of every app that captures the screen. Tyto uses it to take a single still image when you press the shortcut. It does not record video, and it does not capture anything in the background.

If you choose a save folder outside your Pictures folder, macOS grants Tyto access to that folder specifically, and Tyto stores that grant so the folder keeps working after a restart.

Third parties

There are none. Tyto contains no advertising, no tracking libraries and no third-party analytics, and it shares no information with anyone.

Children

Tyto collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date. Any change that affects data handling will also be described in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else, can go to the issue tracker.
