Tyto

Takes a screenshot the instant you press the shortcut, then gets out of your way.

Coming to the Mac App Store Free · macOS 26 or later · Apple silicon

Shortcut, drag, mark up, copy.

What it does

Press ⌘⇧9 and the screen freezes immediately, so a menu, a tooltip or an animation stays exactly where it was while you choose what to keep. Drag a region, or click a window to grab it precisely.

  • Mark it up in place: arrows, boxes, ellipses, lines, text, pixelation and numbered steps
  • Press ⏎ to copy, then paste anywhere. ⌘S saves a PNG instead
  • Every capture is also saved as a PNG to ~/Pictures/Tyto, with the last 24 in the menu bar. Turn it off in Settings
  • Configurable shortcut, and default tool, colour and thickness

Privacy

Tyto makes no network connections and collects no data. Your captures never leave your Mac. The source is on GitHub if you would rather check than take my word for it. The full statement is on the privacy page.

Requirements

macOS 26 or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon. Tyto needs Screen Recording permission, which macOS requires of every app that captures the screen. Grant it in System Settings under Privacy & Security, then relaunch Tyto.
